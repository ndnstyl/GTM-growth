The review loop

The seat that builds never grades its own work. A different seat checks it, the check is bounded, and a halt is a valid ending. That is how a number gets trusted.

Writer is not verifier

Reading back what you just wrote is not verification; it shares every blind spot of the writer. So the harness separates the seats. Junior and builder seats produce. Senior and reviewer seats are read-only: they cannot edit, so they cannot quietly fix what they should have sent back.

Three verdicts

Pass

The work meets the spec and every check reproduced.

Pass with conditions

It can proceed; the named conditions must be closed.

Block

It goes back down with the failing items named. The reviewer never edits the work.

The accuracy hierarchy

For anything that produces a number someone will act on, three levels, each gated by the one above:

  1. Research. Pulls and proves the inputs: control totals present, no empty window, schema unchanged. It never computes the result.
  2. Report. Computes from proven inputs only, then recomputes one figure a second way and shows both.
  3. Senior. Ties out from a different pull than the one the writer used and signs the day. Only this seat may call work done.

A matrix of named checks is the artifact that says "done": every row passes, or the work is incomplete. Nothing rounds to done.

Bounded, and loud at the end

The loop has a stopping rule, not a clean-round target. Three rounds, then it halts and reports with the matrix attached. A halt is a valid exit. A widened tolerance, a dropped check or "close enough" is not.

Before the build: plan convergence

For a high-stakes plan, the plan-convergence skill dispatches two or three independent reviewers in parallel, each through a different lens (feasibility, risk, completeness). Only high-severity findings block. The plan is revised and re-reviewed until no high-severity finding is open or three rounds are spent. If the cap hits with findings open, they are reported to you, not shipped.

Checkers sample, builders fix

The strongest tier is spent on checking, not on building. A checker spot-checks, sends defects back to the builder seat, and does not redo the build. The maximum-effort setting is reserved for authentication, payments, consent, migrations and platform writes.
