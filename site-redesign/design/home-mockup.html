My story

Twenty years of growth marketing, now building AI infrastructure under it.

I am brought in when leadership senses something is wrong and cannot say why. My job is to find what is actually causal, keep what compounds and scrap what only looks productive.

Economic durabilityDecision clarity under uncertaintySystems that explain themselves
01 / INSIDE THE SYSTEM

Nearly two decades in growth systems that look fine and quietly fail

Pattern recognition, not channels or tactics, is what the career is built on.

Teams are usually busy and the reporting reassuring while outcomes decay. The work starts by asking what is actually causal.

02 / HIGH-STAKES FIELDS

Legal, professional services, SaaS and other high-constraint work

Places where being wrong compounds quickly and belief is more dangerous than doing nothing.

In those rooms the job is to slow the system down, identify what drives outcomes, preserve what compounds and remove what only looks productive.

03 / DIAGNOSE AND REBUILD

Find where the reporting is comforting, then rebuild so performance is explainable

Hard calls made early, with economic justification and operational clarity.

Some parts work for reasons no one fully understands. Some are protected because they feel validating. Most reporting tells a comforting story. The craft is telling which is which, saying no to popular ideas and re-architecting deliberately when it is needed.

04 / ORCHESTRATION

Systems that survive misuse

Automation treated as infrastructure: one responsibility per workflow, failure expected and surfaced.

Clear contracts between systems, recovery paths that do not need tribal knowledge, and a rule that if the original builder must maintain it, it is not finished. Boring systems survive and scale.

05 / NOW

AI infrastructure under the growth work

A full go-to-market stack running in production, now offered as a fractional CAIO and CTO.

72 live systems across ten stages: AI seats, playbooks, scheduled loops and always-on systems, treated as probabilistic services behind deterministic guardrails.
