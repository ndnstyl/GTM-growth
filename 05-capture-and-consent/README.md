# 05 · Capture & Consent

Every consent sealed with its exact wording, provable on demand.

## Agents

- **Shows and events seat**: Covers an events manager's job: show playbook, staffing kits and lead follow-up

## Loops

- **Internal do-not-call program**: Daily suppression list fed by text and call stop requests, with a nightly audit of the evidence
- **Event show calendar sync**: Keeps the home-show schedule and its lead sources in step automatically

## Automations

- **Outside consent register**: An independent store of record that seals every consent and opt-out with its exact wording, replays and reconciles it against every vendor nightly, and answers "prove it" on demand
- **Messenger and Instagram desk**: One inbox where the team answers social DMs
