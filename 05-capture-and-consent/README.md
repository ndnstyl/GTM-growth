# 05 · Capture & Consent

Consents and opt-outs recorded with their exact wording and reconciled nightly.

## Agents

- **Shows and events seat**: Answers events questions from a show playbook and drafts staffing kits and follow-up plans; recommend-only

## Loops

- **Internal do-not-call program**: Daily suppression list fed by text and call stop requests, with a nightly audit of the evidence
- **Event show calendar sync**: Pushes the home-show schedule into the CRM event list automatically

## Automations

- **Outside consent register**: An independent register records consents and opt-outs with their exact wording, seals each entry, reconciles against vendor records nightly and can retrieve the record on request
