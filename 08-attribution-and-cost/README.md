# 08 · Attribution & Cost

Every dollar of spend proven before it reaches the CRM.

## Agents

- **Cost accuracy crew**: Research, report and senior review seats prove every platform's spend before anyone reports it
- **Marketing efficiency pacing analyst**: Tracks spend as a share of revenue by channel and office and warns before a target is missed

## Loops

- **Daily cost sync and gap alert**: Every platform's spend is recorded daily, and any gap is emailed with what to do
- **Agency spend canary**: Daily check that agency-reported spend matches the record
- **Monthly marketing-cost check**: Closes each month's marketing cost against revenue, with a month-to-date pull
- **Ad call to lead match**: Ties every ad-driven phone call to its lead daily
- **Contact attribution coverage**: Measures daily how many contacts carry a known source
- **CRM marketing feed and reconcile**: Lands CRM lead and sales data daily and reconciles it against ad data
- **AI spend meter**: Measures what every AI workload costs, daily
