Dana: "Why did we spend a full week's budget in two days? Nobody touched it."
Assistant: "The campaign's budget setting was on automatic and raised itself on the weekend traffic. The weekly cap in the card was a note, not a setting."
Dana: "Then the cap is a rule the assistant enforces: it stages, it never pushes. I apply anything that changes spend."
