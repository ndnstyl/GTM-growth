# Customers Hear Plain Words, Never Our Job Codes

Dana's ruling after a text went out saying "your WO-4410 is scheduled". The customer had no idea what that was.

**Rule:** say what the thing is: "your tune-up is booked for Tuesday morning". Codes live in the scheduling software and in the agent's own working files, never in prose a person reads.

**Applies to:** texts, emails, the board's customer-facing lines, and every report. The plugin's name-the-thing rule is this ruling written for any operator.
