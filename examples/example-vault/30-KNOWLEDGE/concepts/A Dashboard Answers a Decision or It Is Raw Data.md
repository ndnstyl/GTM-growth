# A Dashboard Answers a Decision or It Is Raw Data

Every chart on the Friday read answers one question the owner will act on ("do we pause this keyword?"). A table with no decision attached is raw data; it goes in an appendix and says so.

**How to apply:** before building a view, write the decision in one sentence at the top. If you cannot, do not build it. Example: [[Cost Per Booked Job by Channel]] exists to decide pause or keep in [[Search Ads Pause Rule]].
