# A Count Without Its Basis Is a Rumor

Last quarter two people reported two booking counts for the same week. One counted calls that became appointments; the other counted appointments that were kept. Both were right and neither said which.

**Rule:** a number is printed with its basis in the same line: what was counted, over which dates, from which system. A count of zero prints its basis too ("0 bookings from 3 clicks"), because zero from an empty feed looks the same as zero from a bad week.
