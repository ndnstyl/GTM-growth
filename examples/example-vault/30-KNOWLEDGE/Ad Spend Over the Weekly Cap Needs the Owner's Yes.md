# Ad Spend Over the Weekly Cap Needs the Owner's Yes

Source: [[raw/session-weekend-spend-spike_2026-03-09]]. A weekend budget doubled on its own because a campaign setting was left on automatic. By Monday it had spent a full week's cap.

**Rule:** the weekly cap in [[Spring Tune-Up Campaign]] is a ceiling. The agent may stage a change (a proposed budget, a keyword list) and may not apply it to a live platform. The owner applies it in the platform's own screen and the log records it.

**Why:** a platform write is outward and not undoable; a local change is. See [[Search Ads Pause Rule]] for the same split in practice.
