# A Booked Job Is Not a Lead Until the Phone Number Is Confirmed

Source: [[raw/session-booked-job-wrong-number_2026-03-16]]. A technician drove to a booked job and nobody answered; the number had one digit transposed. Four other bookings that week had the same problem.

**Rule:** the call sheet has a "number read back" box. A booking without it ticked is a tentative hold, and it does not count as booked in [[Cost Per Booked Job by Channel]].

**How it runs:** [[SOP-Missed Call Callback]] repeats the read-back on every return call.
