# Call Coverage by Hour

Source: [[raw/source-call-volume-export_2026-04-06]] (a fictional export). Counted as calls that rang more than four times with no pickup, grouped by the hour the call began, over four weeks.

**Finding:** the gap is the late afternoon and early evening. Midday is covered.

| Hour | Calls | Not answered |
|---|---|---|
| 8 to 11 | 212 | 9 |
| 11 to 15 | 188 | 11 |
| 15 to 18 | 164 | 38 |
| 18 to 20 | 71 | 41 |
