# Cost Per Booked Job by Channel

**Definition:** ad spend for the channel in the week, divided by jobs booked in that week whose first touch was that channel. A booked job has a confirmed phone number ([[A Booked Job Is Not a Lead Until the Phone Number Is Confirmed]]).

**Counted how:** spend from the platform's weekly export; bookings from the scheduling software; joined on the booking's source field. Weeks run Monday to Sunday. A channel with fewer than five bookings in the week prints "too few to read", not a ratio ([[A Count Without Its Basis Is a Rumor]]).

**Used by:** [[SOP-Weekly Ad Spend Check]], [[Spring Tune-Up Campaign]], [[Search Ads Pause Rule]].

Only the owner changes this definition. An agent may propose a change with the number it would produce beside the current one.
