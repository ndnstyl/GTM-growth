# Example vault: Harbor & Pine Home Services (fictional)

Every company, person, number and incident here is invented. The structure is real: this is what the starter vault looks like after a few weeks of use.

Start at [[MEMORY]], the index the assistant loads every session. Then read in this order:

1. [[NORTH-STAR]] for the priorities and [[_BOARD]] for status.
2. One hard rule from its source to its consequence: [[raw/session-weekend-spend-spike_2026-03-09]] became [[Ad Spend Over the Weekly Cap Needs the Owner's Yes]], which shaped [[Search Ads Pause Rule]] and the [[Spring Tune-Up Campaign]] card.
3. One measurement with its counting basis: [[Call Coverage by Hour]] and [[Cost Per Booked Job by Channel]].
4. One SOP in three parts: [[SOP-Weekly Ad Spend Check]].

The log shows what was ingested and distilled, and when: [[log]].
