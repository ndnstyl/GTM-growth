# 02 · Positioning & Offer

Channel strategy, brand voice and offer tests set before a dollar is spent.

## Agents

- **Growth marketing lead**: Plans channel strategy, budget split and offer tests as recommendations; a person applies every change

## Skills

- **Brand voice system**: Produces a tone-of-voice and brand guide every content tool follows
- **Founder voice transfer**: Rewrites drafts so they sound like the founder, with AI tells removed
- **Copy variation burst**: Writes distinct hooks, headlines and offers to test, not reworded copies
- **Short-form strategy playbook**: Picks the audience, scores ideas and sets calls to action so reels bring leads, not just views
