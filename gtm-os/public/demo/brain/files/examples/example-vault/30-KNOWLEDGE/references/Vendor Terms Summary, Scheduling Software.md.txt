# Vendor Terms Summary, Scheduling Software

Source: [[raw/source-scheduling-software-terms_2026-03-02]] (fictional).

- The nightly export includes job status, booking source, customer name, phone and address.
- The export may be used for our own reporting and automation; it may not be resold.
- Deletion on request: the vendor deletes customer data within thirty days of written notice.
- Renewal: annual, owner is Dana, notice window sixty days.

**Why it matters:** [[Review Request Automation]] reads the export, so customer phone numbers are personal data and stay on our own machines, never in a chat window outside the approved tools.
