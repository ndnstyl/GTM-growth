# Missed Call Callback Window

Sam's ruling: a missed call during open hours is returned within thirty minutes, and the return attempt is logged whether or not anyone answers. A call that rang out after hours is returned first thing the next morning.

**Why:** the first company to answer wins the job; the data in [[Call Coverage by Hour]] shows most missed calls cluster in two hours.
