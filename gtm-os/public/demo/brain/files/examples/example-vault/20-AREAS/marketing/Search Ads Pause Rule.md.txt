# Search Ads Pause Rule

A keyword that has spent $120 (fictional figure) with zero booked jobs is a pause candidate. The agent lists candidates in the Friday check; it never pauses anything itself, because pausing changes a live ad platform ([[Ad Spend Over the Weekly Cap Needs the Owner's Yes]]).

**Why $120:** at the current booking rate, that spend would have produced about two bookings by chance. Zero is a signal, not noise.

**How to apply:** read the Friday numbers ([[Cost Per Booked Job by Channel]]), list candidates with their spend and click count, send the list to Dana. Dana pauses in the platform's own screen and the change goes in the [[Spring Tune-Up Campaign]] log.
