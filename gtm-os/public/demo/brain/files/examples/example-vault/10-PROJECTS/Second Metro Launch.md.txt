# Second Metro Launch

**Goal:** Eastvale taking booked jobs with the same answer rate and cost per booked job as Northport.

## Gate
Nothing launches until two things hold for four weeks: evening calls are answered ([[Call Coverage by Hour]]) and cost per booked job is stable in Northport ([[Cost Per Booked Job by Channel]]). This is priority four in [[NORTH-STAR]].

## Playbook
- Reuse the booking page, the confirmation text and the review request ([[Review Request Automation]]).
- Start search only, with the pause rule from day one ([[Search Ads Pause Rule]]).
- Call routing: Eastvale numbers ring the same team; no new phone vendor.

## Status
Waiting on the gate. Reviewed again at the end of the quarter.
