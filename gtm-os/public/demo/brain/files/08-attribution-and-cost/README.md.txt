# 08 · Attribution & Cost

Daily spend recorded and checked against platform controls.

## Agents

- **Cost accuracy crew**: Research, report and senior-review seats check each platform's spend inputs and recompute totals before anything is reported
- **Marketing efficiency pacing analyst**: Tracks spend as a share of revenue by channel and office and warns before a target is missed

## Loops

- **Daily cost sync and gap alert**: Every platform's spend is recorded daily and a daily check lists gaps
- **Monthly marketing-cost check**: Closes each month's marketing cost against revenue, with a month-to-date pull
- **Ad call to lead match**: Ties every ad-driven phone call to its lead daily
- **Contact attribution coverage**: Measures daily the share of contacts that carry a known source
- **CRM marketing feed and reconcile**: Lands CRM lead and sales data daily and reconciles it against ad data
- **AI spend meter**: Meters what each AI workload costs, with a daily reconciliation that is currently incomplete
