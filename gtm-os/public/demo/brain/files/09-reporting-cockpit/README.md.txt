# 09 · Reporting Cockpit

One screen where the numbers refresh themselves.

## Agents

- **Marketing analyst**: Builds report packs and answers "what does this number mean"

## Skills

- **BI workbench standard**: Holds every new report to the interactive standard

## Loops

- **Self-refreshing reports**: Rebuilt hourly, nightly and weekly, with recent days restated as late data lands
- **Daily reporting alert**: Morning alert when a number is late or looks wrong
- **Digital marketing daily record**: Logs each day's changes and decisions and rolls them into a Friday report

## Automations

- **GTM OS cockpit**: One command center where marketing, sales and ops numbers live and refresh themselves
- **BI workbench**: Reports can be pivoted, filtered, drilled into, charted and saved as views
- **Digital marketing board**: In-house ad spend and results by market, platform and campaign, refreshed daily
- **Marketing initiatives tracker**: Every open initiative, with its owner and status, in the cockpit
- **Cockpit chat**: Ask questions of the company knowledge base in plain English and get answers with sources
