# Tech Stack (template: fill in for your business)

This file is the one place the agents learn what your business actually runs on. Delete what you do not use. An agent that does not know your stack guesses, and guesses cost money. Keep it short and dated; re-check it when a platform changes.

## Working environment
- Operating system, editor, the assistant harness (this plugin), the vault location.
- Language runtimes and package managers you allow (name versions).

## Platforms (one line each: what it is, who owns the account, how agents reach it)
- **CRM / system of record:** [name, tenant, API access, read-only or read-write].
- **Call and form tracking:** [name, account].
- **Ads and analytics:** [ad platforms, analytics, search console]. Say for each whether the harness is read-only.
- **Comms:** [mail, calendar, chat]. Say whether agents may draft, never send.
- **Website and hosting:** [CMS, host, deploy path].
- **Warehouse:** [database, schemas, the freshness and coverage of each table you quote].

## Deliverable conventions
- Document formats, who sees what (HTML local, PDF for email), brand files.
- Video, slides and design tooling.

## Coverage notes
When a table's coverage is partial, record the measured coverage, the window, and the date you measured it. Name the table before quoting a coverage figure; two tables in one domain rarely have the same gaps.
