# 07 · Sales Floor

Every call transcribed and scored daily, with training on tap.

## Loops

- **AI call listening**: Every sales call is pulled, transcribed and scored daily, on both the rep's side and the caller's
- **Call floor diagnostics**: Daily board of dials, connects and set rate by rep

## Automations

- **Training classroom**: Self-serve onboarding and product training for the floor
- **Call center knowledge base**: Reps get instant, sourced answers, and the base learns nightly
- **Meeting notetaker**: Local meeting notes and action items that never leave the machine
