# 04 · Paid Media

Every platform's results land daily, and sales flow back so ads optimize for revenue.

## Agents

- **Paid media operator**: Audits accounts, pulls performance and drafts changes across Google, YouTube, Meta and Microsoft; a person applies every live change

## Skills

- **QR campaign builder**: Print-ready, scan-verified QR codes for mail, print and events, each one tracked

## Loops

- **Daily ad performance pull**: Spend, clicks and conversions from every platform land in one warehouse each morning
- **Sending sales back to ad platforms**: Each day the ad platforms are told which leads became sales (Google and Meta), so they can optimize for revenue
- **Weekly paid hygiene read**: Catches waste, broken tracking and policy risk across accounts every week
- **Self-serve social link tagging**: Social posts logged in a shared sheet get tracked links generated automatically; rows that break the naming rules are refused
